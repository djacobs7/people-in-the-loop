Episode 1: Can AI be your therapist?

0:00 18:12

A therapist and an AI developer, friends since childhood, argue about whether AI is coming for mental health. Elana explains where chatbots genuinely help (logic-based work like CBT, practice between sessions, people too nervous to see a human) and where they're dangerous: they agree with you when a good therapist would call you out, and they miss real risk. Daniel weighs in from the builder's side on training, privacy ("everything leaks to a megacorporation"), and what we owe kids. They end on what AI may never do: be a human who cares about you.
